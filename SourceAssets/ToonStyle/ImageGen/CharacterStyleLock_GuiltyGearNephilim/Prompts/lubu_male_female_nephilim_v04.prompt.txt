Create one clean two-character concept sheet for T66 character model reference.

Goal for this revision:
- Return to the v02 visual language: premium smooth anime fighting-game cel shading, dimensional armor forms, crimson / purple / black / gold palette, strong Lu Bu-inspired warlord silhouettes.
- Do not follow the v03 direction. Do not use porcelain-white skin, flat cartoon skin, black axe-like weapon silhouettes, or a changed simplified anime style.
- Make only these changes from v02: slightly fairer skin, bigger heads, fewer clothing ridges/details, simpler weapon details, and remove the vertical cloth panel hanging down the center of the body/crotch.

Subject:
- Left character: original adult male warlord inspired by the historical Lu Bu archetype, not a copy of any existing game character.
- Right character: original adult female warlord inspired by the same Lu Bu archetype, not a copy of any existing game character.
- Both characters are towering nephilim giants with heroic "giga chad" silhouettes: very broad shoulders and chest, extremely narrow waist, long powerful limbs, large readable heads, confident model-reference posture.
- The adult female version has an exaggerated giant-bust heroic silhouette under a fully clothed simplified armored bodice, with the same narrow waist and giant warrior proportions. No nudity, no lingerie, no pin-up pose.

Pose requirements:
- Both characters stand in a clean rigging-friendly A-pose: feet planted shoulder-width apart, torso straight, head facing forward, arms angled down and outward around 35 to 45 degrees from the body, elbows mostly straight, hands clear and visible.
- Leave clear gaps between arms and torso for rigging reference.
- Hands must be readable; fingers should be simplified but correctly wrapped when gripping the weapon.
- Do not use action poses, crossed arms, bent fighting stances, contrapposto, weapon-swinging poses, or hands hidden behind armor.

Head and skin requirements:
- Heads must be bigger than v02, with larger readable face planes and clearly readable eyes, brows, and mouth.
- Skin should be slightly fairer than v02 but still alive and warm in the same polished fighting-game cel-shaded style: fair peach / light ivory skin with warm shadows, not pure white, not porcelain, not gray, not desaturated.

Clothing simplification:
- Keep the same high-quality Guilty-Gear-like cel-shaded armor style from v02, but reduce the number of ridges, seams, bevels, and small trim lines.
- Use broad simplified armor panels and large color blocks: red, royal purple, black, and limited warm gold accent bands.
- Remove the long vertical cloth strip / loincloth / sash panel hanging down the center of the crotch and middle body. The pelvis area should be clean, with no central dangling front tab.
- Side cloth panels or short side cape pieces are okay, but do not place a long cloth panel between the legs.
- Gold trim should be fewer and thicker, not many thin outlines on every edge.
- No dense armor plates, no stacked skirt layers, no filigree, no tiny ornaments, no realistic fabric weave.

Weapon requirement:
- Both characters hold a large simplified halberd / fang tian ji weapon.
- Keep the elegant v02 halberd identity, but reduce decorative clutter: fewer blade ridges, fewer inner cutouts, no gems, no extra tiny prongs, no filigree.
- The weapon must still look like a Lu Bu halberd, not a plain axe.
- The weapon must be fully visible and not cropped.
- Each halberd is held vertically beside the body, slightly outside the A-pose arm line, so the silhouette and rigging pose remain readable.
- The female must grip the halberd shaft correctly with one visible hand wrapped around it, with the shaft passing through the center of the closed hand. No floating weapon, no open hand hovering near the weapon, no loose fingertips.

Style:
- Smooth anime fighting-game cel shading close to Guilty Gear shading language: clean hard-edged highlight bands, smooth simple forms, bold flat color zones, sharp graphic shadows, no painterly brush texture.
- Keep the characters scary and mythic, but not gory: intimidating faces, sharp brows, severe mouths, giant warlord energy.
- Keep both characters clearly from the same game and same faction.

Composition:
- Full-body front view or very slight front three-quarter view, both characters standing on the same ground line.
- White or very light neutral background only.
- No environment, no text, no labels, no logo, no watermark, no frame.
- Clean asset-reference spacing, generous padding around weapons and feet, no cropped body parts.
- Pixal3D-friendly: crisp silhouette, readable limbs, clean separation between weapon and body, no smoke, no glow, no heavy cast shadow, no transparent materials.

Negative constraints:
- Do not make them chibi, cute, soft mascot-like, rubbery, or overly cartoony.
- Do not make the skin pure white, gray, porcelain, chalky, tan, bronze, or brown.
- Do not change the style toward flat simple cartoon art.
- Do not use small heads or realistic head proportions.
- Do not include a long center crotch cloth panel, front loincloth tab, hanging middle sash, or fabric strip between the legs.
- Do not use ornate armor, many thin gold edge lines, layered decorations, busy weapons, gems, or complex prongs.
- Do not use action poses or fashion poses.
- Do not imitate a specific copyrighted character design.
