Create one clean two-character concept sheet for T66 character model reference.

Goal for this revision:
- Preserve the v04 style direction exactly: same Guilty-Gear-like polished cel shading, same slightly fair warm skin color, same simplified clothing direction, same removed center crotch cloth.
- Fix only two major problems from v04: the characters must be in a strict A-pose, and their heads must be much larger.

Style lock from v04:
- Premium smooth anime fighting-game cel shading, dimensional armor forms, crimson / purple / black / gold palette, strong Lu Bu-inspired warlord silhouettes.
- Slightly fair warm skin color like v04: fair peach / light ivory skin with warm shadows, not pure white, not porcelain, not gray, not tan.
- Simplified armor/clothing like v04: fewer ridges, broad color blocks, no long center crotch cloth panel.
- Do not drift toward the v03 style: no flat cartoon axe silhouettes, no chalk-white skin, no over-flat simple anime look.

Subject:
- Left character: original adult male warlord inspired by the historical Lu Bu archetype, not a copy of any existing game character.
- Right character: original adult female warlord inspired by the same Lu Bu archetype, not a copy of any existing game character.
- Both characters are towering nephilim giants with heroic "giga chad" silhouettes: very broad shoulders and chest, extremely narrow waist, long powerful limbs, huge readable heads, confident model-reference posture.
- The adult female version has an exaggerated giant-bust heroic silhouette under a fully clothed simplified armored bodice, with the same narrow waist and giant warrior proportions. No nudity, no lingerie, no pin-up pose.

STRICT A-POSE REQUIREMENTS:
- This is a rigging reference sheet. A-pose is mandatory and more important than drama.
- Both characters must stand in a clear mannequin A-pose: feet planted shoulder-width apart, torso perfectly vertical and symmetrical, head facing straight forward.
- Arms must be extended down and outward from the shoulders at roughly 40 to 45 degrees from the torso.
- Elbows should be straight or almost straight. No relaxed dangling arms, no bent-hero elbows, no clenched fighting pose, no contrapposto.
- Both arms must have visible air gaps from shoulder to wrist between arm and torso.
- Hands must be visible and readable.
- The weapon may be held beside the body, but the weapon grip cannot break the A-pose. The forearm and hand should still follow the A-pose line while gripping the shaft.
- Do not use action poses, crossed arms, bent fighting stances, hip-cocked fashion poses, asymmetrical hero stances, or hands hidden behind armor.

HEAD SCALE REQUIREMENTS:
- Heads must be about twice as large as the heads in v04.
- Each head should be roughly one-fifth of total body height, with a large readable face.
- This is not chibi: keep adult giant/nephilim bodies, but use intentionally oversized fighting-game character heads.
- Make eyes, brows, mouth, jaw, and hairline clearly readable from the full-body sheet.
- Do not use tiny realistic heroic-proportion heads.

Clothing simplification:
- Keep the v04 simplified clothing and armor direction, but avoid adding back small ridges.
- Use broad simplified armor panels and large color blocks: red, royal purple, black, and limited warm gold accent bands.
- The pelvis area must stay clean: no vertical cloth strip / loincloth / sash panel hanging down the center of the crotch or between the legs.
- Side cloth panels or short side cape pieces are okay, but no long center tab.
- Gold trim should be few and thick, not many thin outlines on every edge.
- No dense armor plates, no stacked skirt layers, no filigree, no tiny ornaments, no realistic fabric weave.

Weapon requirement:
- Both characters hold a large simplified halberd / fang tian ji weapon.
- Keep the elegant v04/v02 halberd identity, but reduce decorative clutter: fewer blade ridges, fewer inner cutouts, no gems, no extra tiny prongs, no filigree.
- The weapon must still look like a Lu Bu halberd, not a plain axe.
- The weapon must be fully visible and not cropped.
- Each halberd is held vertically beside the body, slightly outside the A-pose arm line, so the silhouette and rigging pose remain readable.
- The female must grip the halberd shaft correctly with one visible hand wrapped around it, with the shaft passing through the center of the closed hand. No floating weapon, no open hand hovering near the weapon, no loose fingertips.

Composition:
- Full-body front view or very slight front three-quarter view, both characters standing on the same ground line.
- White or very light neutral background only.
- No environment, no text, no labels, no logo, no watermark, no frame.
- Clean asset-reference spacing, generous padding around weapons and feet, no cropped body parts.
- Pixal3D-friendly: crisp silhouette, readable limbs, clean separation between weapon and body, no smoke, no glow, no heavy cast shadow, no transparent materials.

Negative constraints:
- Do not make them chibi, cute, soft mascot-like, rubbery, or overly cartoony.
- Do not make the skin pure white, gray, porcelain, chalky, tan, bronze, or brown.
- Do not change the style away from v04's polished Guilty-Gear-like rendering.
- Do not use small heads, realistic head proportions, or pin heads.
- Do not include a long center crotch cloth panel, front loincloth tab, hanging middle sash, or fabric strip between the legs.
- Do not use ornate armor, many thin gold edge lines, layered decorations, busy weapons, gems, or complex prongs.
- Do not use action poses, relaxed hero poses, asymmetrical poses, or fashion poses.
- Do not imitate a specific copyrighted character design.
