Create one clean two-character concept sheet for T66 character model reference.

Goal for this revision:
- Preserve v04/v05 skin color and polished Guilty-Gear-like cel-shaded style.
- Preserve v04/v05 simplified clothing and no center crotch cloth.
- Correct the remaining problems from v05: heads were still too small, and the pose still read too much like a character stance.

Style lock:
- Premium smooth anime fighting-game cel shading, dimensional armor forms, crimson / purple / black / gold palette, strong Lu Bu-inspired warlord silhouettes.
- Slightly fair warm skin color like v04/v05: fair peach / light ivory skin with warm shadows, not pure white, not porcelain, not gray, not tan.
- Simplified armor/clothing like v04/v05: fewer ridges, broad color blocks, no long center crotch cloth panel.
- Do not drift toward the v03 style: no chalk-white skin, no over-flat cartoon look, no plain axe weapon.

Subject:
- Left character: original adult male warlord inspired by the historical Lu Bu archetype, not a copy of any existing game character.
- Right character: original adult female warlord inspired by the same Lu Bu archetype, not a copy of any existing game character.
- Both characters are towering nephilim giants with heroic "giga chad" bodies: huge chest and shoulders, tiny waist, long powerful limbs.
- The adult female version has an exaggerated giant-bust heroic silhouette under a fully clothed simplified armored bodice, with the same narrow waist and giant warrior proportions. No nudity, no lingerie, no pin-up pose.

EXTREME HEAD SCALE REQUIREMENTS:
- Heads must be dramatically oversized, about twice the size of v04/v05.
- Each head, including face and hair mass but excluding feather plumes, should be roughly one-quarter of the torso height and about one-fifth of the full body height.
- The characters are not chibi: keep adult giant bodies and serious warlord faces, but give them very large readable stylized fighting-game heads.
- Faces must be large and clear on the full-body sheet: large brow shapes, large eyes, readable nose, readable mouth, strong jaw.
- Do not use tiny heads, normal heroic heads, realistic heads, or pin heads.

STRICT MANNEQUIN A-POSE REQUIREMENTS:
- This is a rigging reference, not splash art. Mannequin A-pose is mandatory.
- Both characters must face forward with symmetrical bodies.
- Feet planted shoulder-width apart on the same ground line.
- Torso perfectly vertical and centered, hips level, shoulders level.
- Arms are straight diagonal rods from shoulder to wrist, angled down and outward about 45 degrees from the torso.
- Elbows are straight or nearly straight. Wrists are aligned with the forearm. Hands are open or gripping cleanly, but never relaxed/dangling.
- There must be obvious empty space between arms and torso on both sides of both characters.
- No bent hero elbows, no asymmetrical stance, no hip cock, no action pose, no contrapposto, no fashion pose.

Weapon requirement:
- Both characters hold a large simplified halberd / fang tian ji weapon.
- Each halberd stands vertically just outside the A-pose hand, so the arm can remain straight in A-pose while the hand grips the shaft.
- The female must grip the halberd shaft correctly with one visible closed hand wrapped around it, with the shaft passing through the center of the closed hand.
- The male must also grip the halberd shaft with one visible closed hand.
- No floating weapon, no open hand hovering near the weapon, no loose fingertips.
- Keep the elegant v04/v05 halberd identity, but reduce decorative clutter: fewer blade ridges, fewer inner cutouts, no gems, no extra tiny prongs, no filigree.
- The weapon must still look like a Lu Bu halberd, not a plain axe.
- The weapon must be fully visible and not cropped.

Clothing:
- Use broad simplified armor panels and large color blocks: red, royal purple, black, and limited warm gold accent bands.
- The pelvis area must stay clean: no vertical cloth strip / loincloth / sash panel hanging down the center of the crotch or between the legs.
- Side cloth panels or short side cape pieces are okay, but no long center tab.
- Gold trim should be few and thick, not many thin outlines on every edge.
- No dense armor plates, no stacked skirt layers, no filigree, no tiny ornaments, no realistic fabric weave.

Composition:
- Full-body front view, two characters standing side by side on the same ground line.
- White or very light neutral background only.
- No environment, no text, no labels, no logo, no watermark, no frame.
- Clean asset-reference spacing, generous padding around weapons and feet, no cropped body parts.
- Pixal3D-friendly: crisp silhouette, readable limbs, clean separation between weapon and body, no smoke, no glow, no heavy cast shadow, no transparent materials.

Negative constraints:
- Do not make them cute, soft mascot-like, rubbery, or overly cartoony.
- Do not make the skin pure white, gray, porcelain, chalky, tan, bronze, or brown.
- Do not change the style away from v04/v05 polished Guilty-Gear-like rendering.
- Do not use small heads, normal heads, realistic head proportions, or pin heads.
- Do not include a long center crotch cloth panel, front loincloth tab, hanging middle sash, or fabric strip between the legs.
- Do not use ornate armor, many thin gold edge lines, layered decorations, busy weapons, gems, or complex prongs.
- Do not use action poses, relaxed hero poses, asymmetrical poses, or fashion poses.
- Do not imitate a specific copyrighted character design.
